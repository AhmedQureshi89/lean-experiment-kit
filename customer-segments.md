# Customer segments

The working list of who the business is for. Name one of these in every hypothesis. Replace the
example rows with your own.

| ID | Segment | Who exactly | What we believe they need | Evidence so far |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seg 1 | Work-from-home coffee drinkers | People who work from home and drink two or more cups a day | Good coffee that does not run out mid-week | 0001: 6 of 8 interviewees described running out |
| Seg 2 | Small offices | Offices of 5–20 people that buy coffee for staff | One supplier, one invoice | None yet |

## Known limits
- No real customer is tagged with a segment yet. A test that reaches people says which segment it
  recruited from; it does not prove that everyone it reached belongs there.
