# Board history

The board (`PROGRESS.md`) is generated from the card headers, so it has no history of its own. When a
fact on the board is **corrected** rather than updated (a wrong count, a mislabelled method, a
decision recorded against the wrong experiment), record the correction here, word for word: the date,
what the board said, what it says now, and why.
